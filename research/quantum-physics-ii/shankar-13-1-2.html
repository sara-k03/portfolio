Shankar Exercise 13.1.2

Problem: Derive the degeneracy formula, Eq. (13.1.18).
$$\sum_{k=0}^{n-1} (2\ell + 1) = n^2 \tag{13.1.18}$$

For each \(n\), there are \(\ell\) values starting at 0 up to \(\ell = n - 1\).

For each \(\ell\), there are \(m\) values from \(-\ell\) to \(+\ell\) (including 0). This gives us \(2\ell + 1\) possible \(m\) values.

That gives us \(\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{n-1} (2\ell + 1)\).

Now we need to show that this equals \(n^2\).

$$ \begin{aligned} \sum_{k=0}^{n-1} (2\ell + 1) &= \sum_{\ell=0}^{n-1}2\ell + \sum_{\ell=0}^{n-1}1 \\ &= 2 \cdot \frac{(n-1)n}{2} + n \\ &= n^2 - n + n \\ &= n^2 \end{aligned} $$
