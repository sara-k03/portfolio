Lesson 8: Asmat Śabdāḥ

Asmat is the stem for the first-person pronoun ("I" / "we"). Its forms follow the same case-and-number pattern as the rāma-śabdarūpa in Lesson 4. Click a form to hide it and quiz yourself, click again to reveal it.

Ekavacana
(singular)		 Dvivacana
(dual)		 Bahuvacana
(plural)		 Usage
aham āvām vayam subject
mām āvām asmān object
mayā āvābhyām asmābhiḥ by / with / through
mahyam āvābhyām asmabhyam for
mat āvābhyām asmat from
mama āvayoḥ asmākam of
mayi āvayoḥ asmāsu in / on
In practice, mama kṛte ("for the sake of me") is more commonly used than the dative mahyam — e.g. mama kṛte phalaṁ ānayati, "[Someone] brings a fruit for me."
