Lesson 13: Etat Śabdarūpāṇi

Etat (from the pronoun stem etad) means "this" and it declines across all three genders. Click a form to hide it and click again to reveal it.

Napuṃsakaliṅga (neuter)

Vibhakti Ekavacana
(singular)		 Dvivacana
(dual)		 Bahuvacana
(plural)		 Usage
Prathamā - nominative etat ete etāni subject
Dvitīyā - accusative etat ete etāni object
Tṛtīyā - instrumental etena etābhyām etaiḥ by / with / through
Caturthī - dative etasmai etābhyām etebhyaḥ for
Pañcamī - ablative etasmāt etābhyām etebhyaḥ from
Ṣaṣṭhī - genitive etasya etayoḥ eteṣām of
Saptamī - locative etasmin etayoḥ eteṣu in / on / at
