Lesson 12: Lṛṭ Lakāra

Lṛṭ Lakāra is the future tense. The table below shows its conjugation across puruṣa and vacana using paṭhiṣyati (from the root paṭh, "to read").

Puruṣa (person) Ekavacana
(singular)		 Dvivacana
(dual)		 Bahuvacana
(plural)
Prathama Puruṣa (3rd person) paṭhiṣyati paṭhiṣyataḥ paṭhiṣyanti
Madhyama Puruṣa (2nd person) paṭhiṣyasi paṭhiṣyathaḥ paṭhiṣyatha
Uttama Puruṣa (1st person) paṭhiṣyāmi paṭhiṣyāvaḥ paṭhiṣyāmaḥ
