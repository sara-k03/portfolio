Lesson 6: Laṭ Lakāra

Laṭ Lakāra is the present continuous tense. The table below shows its conjugation across puruṣa (grammatical person) and vacana (number) using gacchati (from the root gam, "to go").

Puruṣa (person) Ekavacana
(singular)		 Dvivacana
(dual)		 Bahuvacana
(plural)
Prathama Puruṣa (3rd person) gacchati gacchataḥ gacchanti
Madhyama Puruṣa (2nd person) gacchasi gacchathaḥ gacchatha
Uttama Puruṣa (1st person) gacchāmi gacchāvaḥ gacchāmaḥ
