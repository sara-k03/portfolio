Lesson 10: Ākārānta Strīliṅga (Rāmā Śabdarūpa)

Akārānta Strīliṅga means feminine words ending in the letter -ā. Rāmā Śabdarūpa is the standard set of noun forms used as the paradigm for this declension class.
